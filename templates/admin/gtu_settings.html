GTU allocation rules

  • Upload complete subject-wise blocks on or before D−1, measured in the examination time zone. No deadline override is enabled.
  • A full block receives an exclusive room. Partial blocks use first-fit decreasing, largest first.
  • Shared rooms cannot contain two blocks of the same subject, exceed the seat limit, or contain more than four blocks.
  • Block identifiers and membership are preserved. Students stay together, ordered by full enrollment number.
  • Each date and shift starts with its own full room pool. Visual edits can move intact blocks or swap whole rooms. Individual seat changes use Custom Mode.
